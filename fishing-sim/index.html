    How to play
    1. Tap the water where you want to cast (or press Space to cast at the drifting marker).
    2. The shadows are real fish, and bigger shadows are rarer ones. Cast just ahead of one to lure it in. Land right on top of it and it gets spooked!
    3. Little nibbles are fake-outs. When the bobber sinks and a ! pops up, tap fast.
    4. Reeling is a chase: steer your net with the arrow keys or WASD (or drag / use the D-pad on a phone) and keep the fish inside it until the bar fills.
    5. Put your catch in your Tank (aquarium), where it swims around, or sell it straight away. Sell fish from the tank whenever you like, then spend coins in the Shop on outfits, buddies, rods and bigger tanks.
    6. Catch more fish to unlock new spots on the Map, each with its own fish. The sky loops from sunrise through daytime, golden hour and sunset to night, and some fish only come out at certain times.
