How to play
  1. Hold anywhere on the water (or Space) to charge a cast. Let go to throw. Further casts reach bigger fish.
  2. Wait. Little nibbles are fake-outs. When the bobber sinks and a ! pops up, tap fast.
  3. Reeling: hold to push the golden glow right, let go and it drifts left. Keep the fish inside the glow until the bar fills.
  4. Sell your catch from the Bucket, then spend coins in the Shop on outfits, buddies, rods and bigger buckets.
  5. The sky loops from golden hour to night. Some fish only come out at certain times. Fill the Fishdex!
