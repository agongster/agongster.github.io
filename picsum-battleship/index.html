How to play

  • Setup: you build your opponent's board — choose their real target photo plus 7 decoys from a pool of 20, then rearrange your fleet (Cruiser 3, Destroyer 3, Frigate 2, Submarine 2, Corvette 2, Scout 1), which starts randomly placed on their 12x12 grid. Drag a ship to move it, or click one to rotate it. They build yours the same way.
  • Fire: on your turn, click any tile on your own board. There's no ammo to choose — a miss reveals a maximally blurry grayscale glimpse, a ship hit reveals a slightly clearer (still grayscale) glimpse, and a fully sunk ship stays grayscale but gets sharper still — it's always at least a little blurry.
  • If your shot lands on one of your opponent's hidden ships, they lose points, and the tile shakes.
  • A hit earns another shot — keep firing until you miss. After any shot, either Guess Now from your 8 candidates or continue. Correct guess: +100 minus 2 per tile revealed so far. Wrong guess: −20, and your turn ends regardless of your streak.
  • Once your board is solved (or fully revealed with no correct guess), you sit out while your opponent keeps going. The game ends when both boards are resolved. Highest score wins!
