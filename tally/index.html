Tally

A shared-expense tracker for the awkward part of group trips. Make a group, log what everyone paid for, split it evenly or by exact amounts, and Tally works out who owes whom — then lets you mark it settled once the money actually moves.

A static page on GitHub Pages talking to a FastAPI backend on Render, backed by a Neon Postgres database. Accounts are real: passwords are hashed with bcrypt on the server and never stored or sent back in plain text.

Just looking around? — it creates a throwaway login and a sample group.
